SHANGHAI / 2050
ARTIST’S NOTES / A WEEK OF URBAN METABOLISM

Where references
meet imagination.

Weather, journeys and information exchanges share a week in one three-dimensional Shanghai. The work brings traceable historical references into conversation with a vision of life in 2050.

Current version: Weather reference from 2–8 June 2025 × an authored 2050 scenario; independent layers share a looping 168-hour timeline.

01 / Three layers

Three rhythms, one city

TIDE

Wind and relative humidity shape the line sea; pale-blue rain follows the reference hourly precipitation. Drop locations, wave crests and lightning geometry are artistic, not measured tides, flood depths or building-level strike locations.

PULSE

Road heat shows scenario pressure while blue and orange activity lights gather and disperse. Place roles, calendars, commuting and reference weather move the hot areas. Silver landmarks remain part of the base even when PULSE is off.

SIGNAL

Ice blue represents machines, computation and exchange services; orange represents public content, community and travel interaction. Relative requests, finite queues and dispatch change active relations, packet counts and slowed display speeds. Lines are authored business relations, not measured fibre routes or electromagnetic trajectories.

02 / Reference and scenario

What is sourced, what is authored

Weather: A cached ERA5 cell near Shanghai, 31.25°N / 121.5°E, supplied by Open-Meteo, covers 2–8 June 2025 in local time. Temperature, relative humidity, precipitation and wind retain their source values; fixed display mappings make them perceptible. This is not a forecast for 2050.

Future operation: Complete road-level weekly congestion observations and building-level network logs were not obtained. Road pressure, public activity and business loads are authored scenarios constrained by geographic and public references, the historical calendar and weather. The future date frames a question about city life rather than a deterministic prediction.

Storm and sound: Public reports document the weekend thunder episode. Exact flashes, branches, locations and thunder sounds are artistic interpretations. Rain sound follows reference intensity. The shared sound control covers weather, music and interface effects.

03 / Exploring the work

Compare one place across a week

Toggle and combine the three layers independently. The 168-hour timeline loops from Sunday into Monday. Pause or scrub to compare a place at different times. Temperature and relative humidity charts retain historical °C and %, replacing the earlier normalized single-day bars.

Select a landmark name or navigation menu to move closer and read its role in the work. Wheel zoom moves smoothly toward the pointer; the widest view returns to the city centre. Drag to orbit and right-drag to pan. Browsers normally require a real click or key press before sound can start.

04 / Geography and vision

Keep the anchors, reveal the memories

The base uses the supplied Shanghai model with locally saved roads, waterways and public facility references. Points, edges and simplified forms make it navigable; this is not a complete, individually certified urban survey.

Silver landmarks are parametric interpretations of architectural features, photographs and supplied concept images. The indoor botanic garden, renewed laneway and Dishui Lake public building are user-approved 2050 concepts. The lakefront roof, skylights and pavilion are authored proposals, not an announced real project. Bounded display exaggeration improves legibility while retaining geographic anchors and metric source forms; enlargement in the image does not assert real construction or height.

Future plans focus mainly on city life: services within reach, rainy-day accessibility, and change that keeps memory and encounter. A smaller final section describes future creative work. These are not official construction plans. Future Echoes adds the audience’s wishes to the work’s shared imagination.

创作札记 / 一周的城市代谢

数据的来处，
想象的去处。

同一座三维上海中，天气、出行与信息交换沿一周时间相互牵动。作品把可追溯的历史参考，与面向2050的生活愿景放在一起。

01 / 三层结构

三种节律，共用一座城市

潮 TIDE

风与相对湿度驱动起伏线海；淡蓝雨丝随参考小时的降水出现。雨滴位置、线场波峰与雷电形态经过艺术处理，不能读取为实测潮位、洪水深度或某栋楼的雷击位置。

脉 PULSE

道路热力呈现各路段情景中的压力，蓝橙活动光点描绘聚集与消散。地点功能、日历、早晚往返与参考天气共同改变热区。银白地标始终属于底图，不依赖脉层开关。

讯 SIGNAL

冰紫蓝表示机器、算力与交换服务，橙金表示公众内容、社区及出行交互。相对请求、有限队列和派发量改变活跃关系、光包数量与艺术慢放速度。连线表达作者设计的业务联系，不是实测光纤路径或真实电磁波轨迹。

02 / 参考与推演

哪些是真实参考，哪些是创作

气象参考：采用上海附近31.25°N / 121.5°E的ERA5单网格历史缓存，经Open-Meteo获得，覆盖北京时间2025年6月2日至8日。温度、相对湿度、降水与风保留原数值；画面只对它们作固定的艺术映射。这不是2050的天气预报。

未来运行：没有取得这一周逐路段完整拥堵观测或逐楼通信日志。道路压力、活动分布和网络负载是现状地理、公共资料、参考日历及天气约束下的作者情景。2050不是确定的预测终点，而是让观看者思考城市如何生活的时间框架。

雷雨与声音：周末雷雨过程有公开气象资料支持；具体闪光时刻、分叉、位置和雷声均为艺术演绎。雨声随参考雨势变化，声音开关统一控制天气音效与原有音乐、界面声音。

03 / 观看方式

在一周里，比较同一处上海

三层独立开关，可任意叠加。底部时间轴覆盖168小时，播放到周日后回到周一；暂停或拖动时间轴可定格比较。温度与相对湿度曲线使用历史参考的°C与%，不再是旧版单日归一化柱图。

点击地标名称或使用定位菜单，镜头会拉近相应区域，地点介绍说明它在作品主题中的角色。滚轮围绕指向位置平滑缩放，缩到最远回到市中心总览；拖动旋转、右键平移。浏览器通常需要一次真实点击或按键，才允许声音播放。

04 / 地理与愿景

保留坐标，让城市记忆发光

底图来自用户提供的上海三维模型，并结合本地保存的道路、水系及公共设施资料。显示采用点、边和简化形体，覆盖程度和身份精度并不等于完整、逐栋认证的城市测绘。

银白地标依据建筑特征、照片与用户概念图制作参数化形体，室内植物园、弄堂改造与滴水湖滨湖公共建筑采用用户确认的2050概念。滴水湖的开孔屋盖、球冠和公共平台为未来设想，不表示已公布的现实建设工程。为便于观赏，显示比例随视距作有限夸张，原地理锚点和米制形体保留；画面上的放大不代表建筑真实增高或扩建。

“未来规划”将城市生活愿景与后续创作放在一起：服务抵达日常、雨天仍可从容往返、更新保留记忆与相遇，并让作品随新的资料与公众经验继续生长。它不是官方建设计划。“未来回声”让观众的愿望成为这一愿景的另一种声音。
