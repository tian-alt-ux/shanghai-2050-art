SHANGHAI / 2050
Artist's notes / data transparency

Where data
becomes an image.

Shanghai 2050 translates environmental rhythms, human activity and information connections into a changing city. Its future is an artistic scenario, not a forecast.

Current version: Synthetic, repeatable scenario curves. No live API or validated future prediction model is connected.

01 / Three layers

A city in three registers

TIDE

Fine moving lines, water-level contours and blue light over humid areas. Relative water levels follow simplified cycles; humidity and wind use synthetic daily variation. The spatial water field is artistic, not a measured surface or flood map.

PULSE

Street blocks, architectural point clouds, blue and orange activity lights, and a moving Huangpu River ribbon. Travel particles follow present-day road geometry and synthetic activity curves. Community, culture and history are themes, not live news; demographic data is not yet connected.

SIGNAL

City particles disperse into abstract clusters as points of light travel along spatial links. The layer imagines collection, correlation and computation; it does not run a live data platform or neural network.

02 / Reading the scene

Time is a score

The charts show relative intensity across one scenario day. Values are normalized and do not represent people, degrees Celsius or metres. Bright bars precede the selected time; faint bars follow it. The vertical rule marks the current moment. Select a chart title to cycle through related topics.

Pause stops time and animation while rotation, zoom and layer selection remain available. Dragging the time slider pauses the scene and reveals the selected environmental and activity state. Browser-generated sound starts off.

03 / Geographic basis

A situated abstraction

Roads, waterways and some building outlines come from a locally saved OpenStreetMap extract, centred on the Huangpu River, Lujiazui and nearby districts. This is not a complete model of Shanghai. The river centreline is smoothed for artistic use and projected locally.

Building footprints become simplified point-cloud volumes. Missing heights and additional buildings use a fixed random seed. Shanghai Tower, Oriental Pearl Tower, Jin Mao Tower and Shanghai World Financial Center are procedural approximations with artistic proportions. Their future appearance is not asserted. If the map cannot load, a schematic road network is clearly identified in the artwork.

01 / 三层结构

同一座城市的三种节律

潮 TIDE

起伏细线、水位轮廓与湿润区域的蓝光。相对水位采用简化周期，湿度与风强度采用合成日变化。空间水位场为艺术构造，不是实测水面或淹没范围。

脉 PULSE

街区、建筑点云、蓝橙活动光点和黄浦江流动光带。出行粒子沿现状路网运动，活动强度采用合成曲线。社区、文化和历史词语为作品主题，不是实时新闻；人口统计数据尚未接入。

讯 SIGNAL

城市粒子散开为抽象群落，光点沿空间连线传播。这一层构想资料采集、关联和计算，并未运行实时数据平台或神经网络。

02 / 观看方式

时间是一份谱

图表显示一个情景日的相对强度。数值经过归一化，不代表人数、摄氏度或米。亮色柱表示所选时刻之前，淡色柱表示之后；竖线标记当前时刻。点击图表标题可轮换同层主题。

暂停会停止时间与动态，仍可旋转、缩放和切层。拖动时间滑块会暂停，并呈现新时刻的环境和活动状态。浏览器合成声音默认关闭。

03 / 地理依据

立足上海的抽象

道路、水系与部分建筑轮廓来自本地保存的 OpenStreetMap 数据，覆盖以黄浦江、陆家嘴及周边中心城区为主，并非上海全市完整模型。黄浦江中心线另作艺术化平滑，投影为局部近似。

建筑轮廓简化为点云体量；缺失高度和补充建筑采用固定随机种子生成。上海中心、东方明珠、金茂大厦及环球金融中心是程序近似形体，比例有艺术处理。未来情景不表示这些建筑在2050年的实际形态。若地图加载失败，主画面会明确标识“示意地图”。
