SHANGHAI 2050 / A WEEK OF URBAN METABOLISM

ONE SHANGHAI.
THREE RHYTHMS.

Rain falls across neighbourhoods. People keep moving. Information travels between them. The work places these ordinary, rarely seen together changes in one navigable Shanghai. Follow a week to see how environment, public life and digital services affect one another.

01 / TIDE

TIDE / Weather leaves a rhythm

Wind and humidity become an undulating field; pale-blue rain follows the reference week. Warm, dry intervals and wet, stormy hours change the same city’s atmosphere.

02 / PULSE

PULSE / Journeys and encounters

Heat along the roads shows scenario travel pressure; blue and orange lights suggest activity gathering and dispersing. Permanent silver landmarks keep memory and everyday life in view.

03 / SIGNAL

SIGNAL / Invisible exchanges

Ice blue represents machines and services; orange represents public content and interaction. Requests, queues and dispatches in different districts become changing packets and light trails.

Independent layers share a looping 168-hour timeline. Weather replays Shanghai-area ERA5 reference data from 2–8 June 2025; traffic, activity and information in 2050 are authored scenarios. Select a landmark to explore a slice of city life.

2050 / CITY VISIONS AND CREATIVE DIRECTIONS

HOW MIGHT WE
LIVE TOGETHER?

Future plans begin here as questions raised by the work. When weather changes the everyday, and people still need to travel and meet, what might a more resilient, accessible and connected Shanghai feel like?

01 / CITY VISION
SERVICES THAT REACH EVERYDAY LIFE

SIGNAL extends beyond central landmarks into outlying areas. The vision for 2050 includes balanced connections among communities, transport hubs and public facilities, with computation and information serving daily needs.

02 / CITY VISION
ROOM TO MOVE IN THE RAIN

TIDE and PULSE meet within the same week. Rain changes scenario road pressure and the choices of where to travel or stay. The work invites attention to rainy-day accessibility, public transport connections and resilient waterfront places.

03 / CITY VISION
CHANGE WITH MEMORY AND ENCOUNTER

From the Bund and lanes to riverfront and cultural places, silver landmarks connect scales of public life. A future city also needs walkable, shared places to pause and meet, keeping its memories visible through change.

04 / NEXT CREATIVE STEPS
A WORK THAT CAN KEEP GROWING

Future creative work could add traceable hydrological and road-level references, refine landmark forms and district differences, and develop rain, thunder and spatial listening. Future Echoes remains a place for people to write their wishes and bring lived experience into later editions. These are directions for development, not claims of completed data integrations.

These city visions are artistic questions, not official construction plans or forecasts. Further development depends on available evidence, audience feedback and artistic choices.

上海，一周的城市代谢。

一场雨如何牵动街区的往返，又如何进入城市的信息网络？《上海2050》把环境、人的活动与服务交换放在同一座三维上海中，让观看者沿着一周的时间，走近一座不断回应变化的城市。

三种彼此牵动的节律

潮 TIDE　风与湿度形成线海，雨丝随参考周天气落下。
脉 PULSE　道路热力与活动光点展现往返、聚集与消散。
讯 SIGNAL　冰紫蓝服务与橙金公众内容，在不同片区之间汇聚、等待和传递。
